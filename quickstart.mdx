This guide walks you through the setup wizard at [app.webglazer.com](https://app.webglazer.com), from creating your account to seeing your first results on the Overview. You need your website address and a payment card for the plan or free trial.

Your first run starts as soon as you finish setup. The engines usually answer within about four to six minutes, and your results appear on the **Overview** when the run finishes. After that, your data updates once a day without you doing anything.

## Before you start

- Have your website address ready, for example `yourbusiness.com`.
- Know the market (country) and language your buyers search in.
- Have a few competitors in mind. WebGlazer suggests some, and you can add your own.

## Set up tracking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.webglazer.com](https://app.webglazer.com) and choose **Continue with Google**, or enter your email and a password and click **Create account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who you are tracking">
    On **Who are you tracking?**, pick **My own brand** to track your own company, or **My clients** if you are an agency.

    This guide follows **My own brand**. Agencies first fill in **Your agency** and then add a first **Client project**. See [Agencies](/setup/agencies).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 1: Project details">
    The **Project details** step opens a screen headed **Brand setup**. Fill in:

    - **Website URL**: your main website.
    - **Brand name**: the name AI answers should use for you.
    - **Market** and **Language**: where your buyers are and the language they search in. WebGlazer may suggest these from your website. Click **Use this** to accept the suggestion.

    Click **Next**. WebGlazer creates your project and starts reading your website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2: Brand understanding">
    WebGlazer reads your site and fills in your **Brand profile**. Check and edit each part:

    - **Description** and **Category**
    - **What you sell**: your products or services.
    - **Who you sell to**: your buyers.
    - **Who you compete with**: suggested competitors. Remove any that are wrong, or use **Add a competitor**.
    - **Question mix**: how much weight to give each buyer stage: **Learning**, **Comparing** and **Buying**. The **About you** switch adds questions that name your brand.

    Click **Next**. Your changes save as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3: Topics">
    On **Review topics**, pick the subjects you want to watch in AI answers. The counter next to **Select topics** shows how many you can keep. Edit a topic name, delete one, or add your own with **New topic**.

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4: Prompts">
    On **Review prompts**, check the questions WebGlazer will ask the AI engines, grouped by topic. Edit or delete any prompt, or type your own in **Add a custom prompt**. The counter next to **Select prompts** shows how many you keep against your limit.

    Click **Looks good**.

    Not sure which prompts to keep? Keep the ones a buyer would really ask, and leave your brand name out of most of them. See [Prompts and topics](/setup/prompts-and-topics#write-prompts-that-measure-something-useful).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 5: Review and choose a plan">
    You see a summary: **Setup complete**, how AI describes you today, example questions, and the competitors you are **Benchmarked against**. Click **See my plan**.

    Pick a plan. You go to Stripe to enter your card. If your account can get the free trial, the Basic plan shows **Start 7-day free trial**. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).

    When checkout is done, Stripe sends you back to WebGlazer to finish the last steps below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your AI engines">
    Back in WebGlazer, **Welcome to WebGlazer** shows the AI engines. On Standard and Advanced it says "All 5 AI models are in your plan" and every engine is on. On Basic it says "Pick up to 3 of the 5 AI models". On an agency plan you pick 1 to 5, and a line shows how many daily credits your choice uses. Click **Continue**.

    You can change engines later. See [Engines and schedule](/setup/engines-and-schedule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions and invite your team">
    Answer the short questions about your role and goals, then **How did you hear about WebGlazer?**.

    On **AI visibility is a team effort**, enter teammates' emails, pick a role, and click **Send invite**, or click **Skip for now**. The roles are **Company Admin**, Member (shown here as **Company Member**) and **Viewer**. See [Team](/setup/team).

    On **Setup complete. You're ready to go.**, click **Explore insights**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your first run">
    Your first run starts now. The **Run Progress** popup opens over the Overview with one ring per AI engine. Each ring fills as answers come in. You can close the popup; the run keeps going.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your results on the Overview">
    When the run finishes, the **Overview** shows **Visibility Over Time** and the **Rankings** table, which compares your brand with your competitors on visibility, sentiment and mention order.

    See [Overview](/dashboard/overview) for how to read each part.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
After the first run, WebGlazer asks the AI engines your prompts once a day for each brand and records the answers. New data shows up after each daily run. You do not need to do anything to keep tracking.
</Note>

## What to do next

1. Go to **Settings** > **Brands** and accept the competitors you want to compare with from **Brand suggestions**. Only your own brand and the competitors you track count in **Rankings**, Share of Voice and the other competitor numbers. A brand AI names that you have not accepted is left out. See [Competitors](/setup/competitors).
2. Open **AI Visibility** > **Prompts** to see each answer and where your brand appears.
3. Invite colleagues from **Settings** > **Members**.
