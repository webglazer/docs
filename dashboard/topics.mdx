The **Topics** tab shows your visibility topic by topic. Use it to see which parts of your market AI already connects you with, and which ones it does not.

Open it from **AI Visibility** > **Topics**.

## Topics Explorer

"Bigger tiles hold more prompts. Darker tiles are topics where AI mentions you more."

### Tile map

- Each tile is one topic with active prompts.
- Tile size is the number of active prompts in the topic.
- Tile shade is your [Visibility](/metrics/visibility) in that topic: the share of the topic's answers that name you, over the selected period and filters. Darker means higher.
- The map draws the top 10 topics by prompt count. The label above it says how many it shows, for example "Top 10 of 14 topics by prompts".
- On a phone, the map becomes a ranked list.

### Visibility trend

Next to the map, one row per topic (the top 8 by prompt count) shows your visibility in that topic from day to day across the period. A day without a run breaks the line. A topic with no runs yet says "No runs yet".

### All topics

With more than 10 topics, **See all topics (N)** swaps the trend for a table of every topic. Search it with the search box, and sort by **Topic**, **Prompts** or **Visibility**. **Show trend** switches back.

## Filtering by topic

Click a tile, a trend row or a table row to set the page's topic filter. The whole of **AI Visibility** then shows only that topic. Click the selected topic again to clear it.

Branded topics are left out of this tab, and the branded filter options are hidden, because branded answers have no visibility rate.

If there are no topics with active prompts, the tab says "No topics with active prompts yet. Add prompts to a topic to see it here."
