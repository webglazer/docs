The **Opportunities** tab turns your citation data into a pick-list: "Get on the sources AI reads". Each card is a site AI cites to answer your questions where you are not mentioned. Get featured there and you can show up in those answers.

Open it from **AI Visibility** > **Opportunities**.

## Which sites become an opportunity

A cited site shows up here only when all of these are true:

- It is a qualifying source: cited by 3 or more engines across 3 or more non-branded prompts in the period. See [Citations](/metrics/citations).
- It is not your own site and not the site of a competitor you declared.
- At least one of its pages has been checked, and no checked page names you.

Only non-branded prompts count, because a branded prompt already names you. The page filters and date range apply, the same as on the **Citations** tab.

## What a card shows

- **The site** (its host name).
- **Priority**: **High priority** when the site is cited for 10 or more of your questions, **Medium priority** otherwise.
- **Why**: a sentence such as "AI cites example.com to answer 4 of your 25 questions, and you're not on it. Get listed here and you're in the answer for all 4."
- **The questions** it feeds: the first 3, then "+N more".
- **Mentioned here instead**: competitors you declared that AI named in the answers citing this site.
- "Back on your list: a newer run still shows this gap." when you marked it done and a later run cites the site again while it still does not name you.

## Acting on a card

- **Add to to-do**: moves the card to your **To-do list** (in the sidebar), where you mark it **Done** or **Remove** it. See [To-do list](/dashboard/todo).
- **Dismiss**: hides the card. To bring a dismissed card back, open **To-do list** > **History** and click **Reopen**.

This tab shows open cards only. When everything is picked or dismissed, it says "Nothing open. Check your To-do list." With no cards at all, it says "No opportunities yet".

Your choices (to-do, done, dismissed) are your own. Other users of the project keep their own list.
