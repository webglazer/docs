The **Reports** page holds every report the WebGlazer team has prepared for the brand you are viewing. Open a report in the dashboard to follow its links to the underlying answers, or download it to share outside WebGlazer.

## Find your reports

Click **Reports** in the sidebar. The list shows each report with its name and **Date**, newest first.

Reports are prepared and added by the WebGlazer team. You cannot create one yourself. If the list says **No reports yet**, the team has not added one for this brand. Reports show up here once they are ready.

Reports belong to a brand. Switch brands in the sidebar to see another brand's reports.

## Open a report

Click **Open** on a report. It opens on its own page inside the dashboard.

- Answers in the report link to the prompt they came from. Clicking one opens that prompt in **AI Visibility** > **Prompts** in a new tab, on the engine that gave the answer.
- Click **Back to reports** to return to the list.

Some reports are spreadsheet files. Those show **Download** in the list instead of **Open** and download the file directly.

If a report cannot be shown, the page says **Report not available**. Go back to the list and try another report.

## Download a report

On an open report, click **Download** to save it as an HTML file you can send to anyone.

<Note>
  The downloaded file keeps the numbers but leaves out the AI answers and the links back into the dashboard. Those parts only work for people who can sign in to your WebGlazer account.
</Note>
