The **To-do list** holds the sources you picked from **AI Visibility** > **Opportunities**: sites AI cites for your prompts where your brand is not mentioned. Use it to track each one until it is handled.

Which sites become an opportunity, and what each card shows, is on the [Opportunities tab](/dashboard/opportunities) page.

## Pick items for your list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Opportunities">
    Go to **AI Visibility** > **Opportunities**. If it says **No opportunities yet**, check again after your next daily run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to work on">
    On each card, click **Add to to-do** to pick it, or **Dismiss** to hide it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work through your To-do list

Click **To-do list** in the sidebar. **Your list** has two views:

- **To-do**: what you picked. Click **Done** when you have handled a source, or **Remove** to send it back to Opportunities.
- **History**: items marked done or dismissed. Click **Reopen** to put one back in Opportunities.

The filter bar sets which runs the list is based on. If the list is empty, click **Pick tasks in Opportunities**.

Your picks and statuses are personal. Teammates on the same brand keep their own list. Every role, Viewers included, can keep a list.

## When a done item comes back

If a newer run still cites the source and it still does not name you after you marked it done, the item goes back to the **Opportunities** tab with the note "Back on your list: a newer run still shows this gap." Pick it again to put it back on your To-do list. Dismissed items stay dismissed.

## Use your AI agent

The To-do list can be filled by an AI agent connected through the WebGlazer MCP server. Until you connect one, a **Connect your AI** card at the top explains how:

1. Generate a token in **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Your AI agent**.
2. Paste it into Claude Code, Cursor, or any MCP client.
3. Ask it for your opportunities. What it picks lands on this list.

Click **Connect your AI** to open **Settings** > **Integrations**, or **Not now** to hide the card until your next browser session. The card disappears for good once you hold an active token. See [MCP server](/integrations/mcp) for setup.
