Settings is where you change how WebGlazer tracks your brand and manage your account. This page explains each section and what you can change there.

## Open Settings

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**. The sidebar switches to the Settings menu. Click **Back to app** to return to the page you came from.

Settings has two groups:

| Group | Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | **Account** | Your credit balance (agency accounts) |
| Account | **Billing & plan** | Your plan, add-ons, payment method and invoices |
| Workspace | **Brand profile** | What AI should know about the current brand |
| Workspace | **Brands** | Your brand and the competitors you track |
| Workspace | **Projects** | Every brand you track, its engines, time zone and prompt use |
| Workspace | **Members** | Your team and their roles |
| Workspace | **Integrations** | Google Search Console and your AI agent (MCP) |

**Brand profile** and **Brands** apply to the brand selected in the sidebar's brand switcher. **Projects**, **Members** and **Integrations** cover the whole company. Some changes need the **Company Admin** role. If you cannot edit a field, the page says so.

## Account

Agency accounts see their credit balance here.

To delete your account or company, email [support@webglazer.com](mailto:support@webglazer.com). There is no delete button in the app.

## Billing & plan

Shows your plan and its renewal or trial end date, lets you add prompts or credits, change or cancel your plan, update your card and download invoices. Only a Company Admin can make changes.

See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing) for every step.

## Brand profile

**Brand Profile** holds what the AI knows about your brand. Your next run uses these answers.

- **Logo**: click **Upload logo** or **Change logo**. Logos must be images under 2 MB.
- **Market and language**: the market and language your buyers search in. Click **Edit** (or **Choose** / **Confirm** if it is not set yet) to change it. New AI prompts and your next run use this. Existing prompts stay as they are. Only a Company Admin can change it.

The right side of the page holds your business details:

- **Description** and **Category**.
- **What you sell**: your products or services. Use **Add a product** to add one.
- **Who you sell to**: your buyers.
- **Question mix**: sliders for **Learning**, **Comparing** and **Buying**. They steer prompts you add later on the Prompts page. They never change prompts you already track. The three values must add up to 100%.

After you edit, a bar appears. Click **Save changes**.

Competitors are not edited here. Use **Brands**.

## Brands

Your brand and the competitors you compare against, plus **Brand suggestions**: brands AI named in your answers that you do not track yet. The **Brands** menu item shows how many suggestions are waiting.

See [Competitors](/setup/competitors) and [Brands](/setup/brands).

## Projects

A table of every brand (project) you track, with its market, active prompts against your plan limit, AI engines, **Frequency** and creation date. Click a project's name to switch to it.

- Click the engines to open **Tracking setup**, turn engines on or off, and click **Update**.
- The row menu has **Edit details** (market, language and **Time zone**), **Tracking setup**, **Export chats** (a CSV of the project's AI answers) and **Delete project**.
- The time zone sets when daily runs happen and which date they count for. Only a Company Admin can change it.
- Deleting a project asks you to type its domain to confirm.

See [Engines and schedule](/setup/engines-and-schedule) and [Brands](/setup/brands). Agency accounts also see **Clients** and **Pitches** tabs here; see [Agencies](/setup/agencies).

## Members

Invite teammates, change their roles and remove access. See [Team](/setup/team).

## Integrations

**Integrations** connects the tools that feed your prompts and the AI clients that read your results.

- **Google Search Console**: connect Search Console to compare Google search data with AI answers and to add prompts from your real search queries. See [Search Console](/dashboard/search-console).
- **Your AI agent**: connect an AI client such as Claude or Cursor to your WebGlazer data through the WebGlazer MCP server. See [MCP server](/integrations/mcp).
